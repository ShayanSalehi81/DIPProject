Why image quality belongs in microscopy reasoning

VLMs see the specimen through an acquisition pipeline

“A correct semantic answer may depend on blur, noise, sampling, compression, registration, and detector response—not only biological content.
Motivated by Micro-Bench’s microscopy errors and Q-Instruct’s low-level vision curriculum.
The missing link

Quality → evidence → answer

Natural-image quality benchmarks rarely model microscopy physics; microscopy VLM benchmarks rarely isolate low-level corruption.

Our response: a traceable, paired benchmark in which every corruption and restoration is known.

Phase 1.5 · Implementation 25 / 32
Uncertainty and paired effects

Report effects, confidence intervals, and transitions—not isolated accuracy

Primary endpoint

Task-macro accuracy

MacroAcc(c) = meantask meansample 𝟙[ŷi,c = yi]

Each task type receives equal weight, preventing frequent tasks from dominating.

10,000× bootstrap

Resample complete triplets; report paired 95% percentile intervals.

Exact McNemar

Test discordant pairs; Holm-adjust the three condition comparisons.

Four effects

Degradation loss, restoration gain, residual vs clean, recovery fraction.

Transition audit

Stable, restored, unrecovered, harmed, helped, transient, only-restored, always-wrong.

Scope boundary: completed Phase 1 annotations support coarse and fine perception only; no cognition category is inferred or fabricated.

What the evidence supports

Three conclusions—and one disciplined boundary

01

A reproducible DIP benchmark

1,701 clean sources become 15,480 controlled recipes with strict train/evaluation separation, full provenance, and 99.95% severity monotonicity.

02

Restoration follows invertibility

Notch filtering and denoising can recover structured/noisy corruption; clipping, quantization, sampling, bleaching, and compression remain information-limited.

03

Fine perception is the stress point

Overall loss is modest and uncertain, but strong corruptions and particular families expose meaningful paired failures that aggregate accuracy obscures.

Boundary: these are controlled synthetic degradations and exploratory n=500 VLM results; pixel improvement does not automatically imply biological or semantic improvement.
